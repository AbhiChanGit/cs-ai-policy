    Method and validity

    Approach

    Qualitative content analysis. One official source per institution (department page first, then college, then university), saved as excerpts with URL and access date. Claude coded each source with the codebook and quoted the sentence behind each label. Tests confirm every quote exists in the saved text.

    Codebook: data/codebook.md. Raw excerpts: data/raw/.

    Agreement checks

    Limitations

    • Convenience sample, weighted to research universities; not representative of all US programs.
    • Sources mix formal policy, guidance pages and news stories; dates range 2023 to 2026.
    • Excerpts were extracted by a fetch tool; a human spot check of 5 URLs is listed as a pending task.
    • One coder (Claude). The keyword rule was written after reading the texts, so it is a consistency check, not an independent coder.
